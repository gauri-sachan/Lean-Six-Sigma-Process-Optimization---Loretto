**Syracuse, NY | September – December 2025**
**Syracuse University, Whitman School of Management**

---

## 🏥 About the Project

This repository documents a Lean Six Sigma consulting engagement conducted at **Loretto Health & Rehabilitation**, a long-term care and rehabilitation facility in Syracuse, NY. The project focused on improving the efficiency and reliability of laundry operations serving residents across two facility buildings — Cunningham and Fahey.

Using the **DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control)** methodology, the team identified root causes of persistent laundry delays and backlogs, implemented low-cost digital solutions, and established a sustainable control framework for ongoing performance monitoring.

## 🔍 Problem

Laundry operations were experiencing frequent delays, backlog formation, and lack of process visibility — driven by Washing Machine 2 downtime, absence of pickup/delivery tracking, and unbalanced staff allocation.

---

## ✅ Solutions Implemented

- **QR Code + Google Form** for digital laundry tracking
- **Tableau Dashboard** for real-time management visibility
- **Over-the-door Laundry Bags** to reduce no-access incidents
- **Washing Machine capacity recommendation** to eliminate the bottleneck
