# Laundry Process Optimization – Lean Six Sigma Project
**Loretto Health & Rehabilitation | Syracuse, NY**

---

## 📌 Project Description (150 words)

This Lean Six Sigma consulting project was conducted at Loretto Health & Rehabilitation (700 E Brighton Ave, Syracuse, NY) to improve the efficiency and reliability of laundry operations serving residents across two facility buildings — Cunningham and Fahey.

Using the DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control) methodology, the team identified persistent delays, laundry backlogs, and lack of process visibility as core operational challenges. Root cause analysis validated that frequent downtime of Washing Machine 2, absence of pickup and delivery tracking, and unbalanced staff allocation were the primary drivers of inefficiency.

Solutions implemented included a QR-code-linked Google Form for digital laundry tracking, a Tableau performance dashboard for real-time management visibility, standardized laundry bags to reduce no-access incidents, and a recommendation for an additional washing machine to eliminate the capacity bottleneck. The project concluded with defined KPIs, SOPs, and ownership transfer to laundry management for long-term sustainability.

## 🔍 Problem Statement

Laundry operations at Loretto Health & Rehabilitation were experiencing frequent delays and backlogs, machine breakdowns, and a lack of reliable data to confirm when laundry was picked up or delivered — resulting in limited process visibility and reactive management.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Reduce laundry backlog and turnaround time
- Improve visibility into laundry pickup and delivery activities
- Establish accountability through standardized tracking
- Optimize staff allocation based on actual workload data
- Provide management with real-time performance insights
- Design a sustainable control mechanism to maintain improvements

---

## 📐 Scope

**In Scope:**
- Laundry pickup and delivery workflow
- Staff clock-in and clock-out tracking
- Washing and drying machine usage and downtime
- Process visibility and performance measurement
- Pilot implementation of a digital tracking solution

**Out of Scope:**
- Procurement of new laundry machines
- Changes to facility infrastructure
- Resident behavior or usage patterns
- Non-laundry-related facility operations
- Linen laundry

## 🔬 DMAIC Summary

### Define
- Mapped end-to-end laundry workflow across Cunningham and Fahey buildings
- Developed SIPOC diagram identifying laundry staff as responsible for all operational steps
- Translated Voice of the Customer into 5 Critical to Quality Characteristics (CTQCs):

| CTQC | Metric |
|------|--------|
| Timeliness | Pickup and return ≤ 4 hours |
| Accuracy | ≥ 95% laundry delivered to correct room |
| Consistency | ≤ 3 unexpected delays per week |
| Backlog Visibility | Backlog days clearly communicated |
| Workload Balance | Hours worked per staff ≤ 40/week |

---

### Measure
- Collected staff clock-in/clock-out data to evaluate workload distribution
- Recorded washing and drying machine cycle times, with focus on Washing Machine 2 downtime
- Collected misappropriation incident data from Loretto (October 2024 – September 2025)
- Developed baseline performance metrics for turnaround time, staff utilization, machine availability, and laundry backlog
- No historical tracking data existed prior to this project — all measurements represent a newly established baseline

**Key Baseline Observations:**
- Machine downtime consistently resulted in backlog formation
- Staff idle time increased during periods of reduced machine availability
- Backlog recovery required extended effort once machines returned to operation

---

### Analyze
Root cause analysis was conducted using process flow analysis, staff workload analysis, bottleneck identification, and a Cause-and-Effect (Fishbone) diagram.

**Validated Root Causes:**
1. Improper staff allocation due to lack of workload visibility
2. Washing Machine 2 downtime and reduced capacity acting as the primary bottleneck
3. Absence of pickup and delivery tracking
4. Lack of real-time process visibility for management

**Fishbone Categories:**
- **People:** Staff allocation imbalance, multitasking across all steps
- **Machines:** Frequent downtime and reduced speed of Machine 2
- **Methods:** No standardized pickup and delivery process
- **Measurement:** Absence of tracking and performance data
- **Environment:** Multiple buildings and 14 floors increasing transport time

---

### Improve
Three integrated solutions were designed and piloted:

**1. Digital Laundry Tracking System (QR Code + Google Form)**
- QR codes placed on resident bathroom doors link to a standardized Google Form
- Fields: Room number, building, floor, laundry status (Picked Up / Delivered / No Access / Family Laundry), staff name, auto-generated timestamp
- Form Link: [Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScCL_8AGKDN62QNxb3OBTiDzfrWj4uTnw2KhqZb6MmOYOqbdw/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=107874309815827761089)

**2. Tableau Performance Dashboard**
- Visualizes pickup vs. delivery status, staff-level activity, no-access patterns, and daily completion rates
- Enables proactive management decision-making and misappropriation incident back-tracking
- Dashboard Link: [Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/kaavya.shastri/viz/LSS-Laundry/Dashboard1?publish=yes)

**3. Laundry Bag Recommendation**
- Over-the-door hanging laundry bags to reduce no-access incidents and improve sorting efficiency

**4. Additional Washing Machine Recommendation**
- Recommended acquisition of an additional industrial washing machine to directly address the capacity bottleneck identified at Washing Machine 2

**Pilot Results:**
- Improved visibility into laundry status
- Reduced ambiguity around pickups and deliveries
- Better staff accountability without punitive oversight
- Faster identification of delays and backlog

---

### Control
Sustainability framework established through:

**KPIs Defined:**

| KPI | Target | Frequency | Owner |
|-----|--------|-----------|-------|
| Laundry Turnaround Time | Within daily service window | Daily | Laundry Manager |
| Pickup & Delivery Completion Rate | ≥ 90% | Daily | Supervisor |
| Machine Availability | ≥ 90% uptime | Weekly | Maintenance / Laundry Manager |
| Tracking Compliance | ≥ 98% | Daily | Supervisor |

**SOPs Established:**
- Laundry Pickup and Delivery SOP
- Machine Operation and Downtime SOP
- Dashboard Review SOP (daily by supervisor, weekly by manager)

**Ownership** transferred to Laundry Manager with oversight from Facility Administration.

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- Process inefficiencies were driven by system design, not individual performance
- Low-cost digital tools (Google Forms + Tableau) achieved meaningful operational improvements
- Real-time visibility enables proactive management and reduces reactive decision-making
- Sustainable improvement requires clear KPIs, SOPs, and defined ownership
